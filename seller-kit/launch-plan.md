# Launch plan: from 0 to 10 sales a day

## The honest math first

**10 sales a day × $9 = $90/day** before fees (Gumroad takes a percentage plus a small fee per sale; check their current pricing).

A well-made $9 product page typically converts **2–5%** of *interested* visitors. So 10 sales a day needs roughly **200–500 targeted visitors a day** on your Gumroad page.

**Gumroad will not send you that traffic on day one.** Its Discover marketplace mostly promotes products that already have sales and ratings. In the beginning, nearly every visitor comes from **you**: your videos, posts and communities. The plan below is built around that. Most creators who reach 10 sales a day get there through **consistent daily content for 4–8 weeks**, not through one big launch.

> Never buy fake reviews or fake sales. Gumroad can ban the account, and buyers notice. Real early buyers plus great support give you real ratings within weeks.

---

## Week 0: Prepare (2–3 days)

- [ ] **Run 3–4 workflows live with your own accounts** (recommended: 02 WhatsApp, 03 Website Chatbot, 05 Lead Finder, 01 Inbox). You'll catch any account-specific surprises and you'll have real results to show.
- [ ] **Record 4 screen videos (30–60 s each).** These are your most valuable marketing assets:
  1. *WhatsApp:* send a voice note, the bot answers in seconds
  2. *Website chatbot:* paste a URL, train, ask a question, get an answer with a link
  3. *Lead finder:* type "dentists in Austin", the Google Sheet fills up with leads and openers
  4. *Inbox:* a new email arrives, a label and a draft reply appear in Gmail
- [ ] Set up the Gumroad page with the copy from `gumroad-listing.md` and the images in `images/`. Put your best video as the first cover.
- [ ] Turn on **Gumroad affiliates** (e.g. 40–50% commission) so others can promote you.
- [ ] Create a **free lead magnet**: publish workflow 10 (Daily AI News Digest) on its own as a free ($0+) Gumroad product that links to the full pack. Free products get downloaded much more, build your email list and lead to upgrades. *(The license allows this because you're the author.)*

## Weeks 2–8: The daily routine (about 60–90 minutes a day)

1. **One short video a day.** A workflow in action, a quick setup tip, a before/after. Short-form video is the cheapest way to reach automation-curious people.
2. **Two value posts** (LinkedIn/X/Threads): a lesson, a mistake, or a real cost breakdown from your own usage ("my WhatsApp bot answered [N] messages for $[X]").
3. **Ten helpful replies** in communities (Reddit, n8n forum, Facebook/WhatsApp groups for automation and no-code). Help first, and link only when relevant and allowed.
4. **Once a week:** a longer YouTube tutorial, e.g. *"Build a WhatsApp AI bot with n8n (free template)."* Link the full pack in the description. Long videos keep selling for months.
5. **Once a week:** pitch 3 people for affiliates (n8n YouTubers, AI-tools newsletters, automation Twitter accounts). Offer a free copy + 40–50% commission.

## After the sale: turn buyers into fans

Use Gumroad's **Workflows** (automated emails):

| When | Email |
|---|---|
| Right after purchase | Welcome + "start with workflow 10, it's live in 3 minutes" |
| Day 2 | "Did your first workflow run? Here are the 3 most common fixes" (link to the FAQ) |
| Day 5 | "Which workflow do you want next?" + ask for a ⭐ rating |
| Day 14 | The setup-call version or a new update, if you have one |

**Ship updates.** Every 4–6 weeks, add a workflow or improvement, bump the version (v1.1, v1.2…) and send a Gumroad update to all buyers. Updates bring buyers back, create social proof and give you something new to post.

---

## What to watch (weekly)

| Metric | Healthy | If it's low |
|---|---|---|
| Page conversion (sales ÷ visits) | 2–5% | Improve the first cover (use a video), the title and the first 3 lines of the description |
| Refund rate | < 5% | Improve the guide where people get stuck; answer support fast |
| Top traffic source | One channel clearly winning | Spend 80% of your time on the channel that sells |

**When you reach 50+ sales and ratings, test raising the price** (e.g. to $19). Higher-priced products often sell almost as often, and Gumroad's Discover ranks proven products higher. Only call $9 a "launch price" if you really raise it later.
