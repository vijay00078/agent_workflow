QUICK START (10 minutes)
------------------------
1. Open n8n (n8n Cloud or your own server; n8n 1.100 or newer, including 2.x).
2. Create a new workflow, open the ⋯ menu and choose "Import from File…".
   Pick a file from the "workflows" folder. Tip: start with
   10-daily-ai-news-digest.json (3-minute setup).
3. Read the yellow START HERE note on the canvas.
4. Connect your accounts (nodes with a ⚠️) and fill in the ✏️ Config node.
5. Click "Execute workflow" to test, then Publish (n8n 2.x) or Activate (n8n 1.x).
