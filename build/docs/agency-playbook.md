# Before you start {#intro}

The 10 workflows in this pack solve problems that small businesses have **every day**:

- slow replies to leads
- WhatsApp messages answered at midnight
- invoices typed by hand
- blogs that never get written

Many of those businesses would happily pay someone to make the problem disappear. That someone can be you.

This playbook shows you a simple, proven way to do that:

1. **Pick a niche** and the 2–3 workflows that matter most to it.
2. **Package them as an outcome** ("every lead answered in 60 seconds"), not as "n8n workflows".
3. **Show a working demo** before you ask for money.
4. **Deliver cleanly** and keep the client on a **monthly care plan**.

::: warning
**Honest note:** there is no guaranteed income here. The prices in this playbook are **examples** of common market ranges. They're not promises. Your results depend on your niche, your market, your effort and how well you follow up. The good news is that every step below is something you can start today, with no budget.
:::

# Package your offers 📦 {#offers}

Sell **one clear outcome** with a **setup fee + monthly care plan**. The monthly part is what turns this into a real business.

## Three example packages

| | **Starter** | **Growth** | **Done-for-you** |
|---|---|---|---|
| What | 1 automation (e.g. the WhatsApp bot) | 2–3 automations that work together (e.g. 06 + 02 + Error Alerts) | Full system, custom tweaks, monthly improvements |
| Setup | 1–3 days | 1–2 weeks | 2–4 weeks |
| Example setup fee (US/UK/EU) | $300–$800 | $1,000–$2,500 | $3,000+ |
| Example monthly care | $100–$200 | $250–$500 | $500–$1,500 |
| Example in India | ₹8k–₹20k + ₹2k–₹5k/mo | ₹25k–₹60k + ₹6k–₹12k/mo | ₹75k+ + ₹15k+/mo |

These are **starting points**, not rules. Price on the **value** for the client (one extra booked patient can be worth more than your monthly fee), and on what your local market accepts.

## What the monthly care plan includes

- Monitoring with the **Error Alerts** bonus workflow. You fix problems before the client notices.
- Keeping credentials and connections alive (Google, Meta, OpenAI).
- Updating the bot's knowledge (new prices, services, FAQs) and **retraining the website chatbot**.
- Tuning the prompts based on real conversations.
- A short **monthly report**: messages answered, leads captured, hours saved.

::: note
**Who pays for AI and hosting?** The simplest and cleanest setup is that the client pays for their own OpenAI account and n8n (Cloud or a small server). You only charge for your work. The client owns the accounts, which builds trust and keeps your margins clean.
:::

# Find your first clients 🔎 {#clients}

## 1. Build a demo before you sell

Nothing sells like seeing your **own business** answered by AI. Two 10-minute demos that work like magic:

- **Website chatbot demo (03).** Put the prospect's website URL in Config, click *Train the bot now* and send them the **Chat URL**: *"I trained an AI assistant on your website. Ask it anything a customer would ask."*
- **Lead finder demo (05).** Run *"[their niche] in [their city]"* and send 10 rows: *"Here are 10 businesses that look like your ideal customers, with a personal opener for each."*

## 2. Build your lead list

Use **workflow 05** on your own niche, e.g. *"dentists in Pune"* or *"real estate agents in Austin"*. Each lead comes with a score, signals (no online booking, slow site, low rating) and an opener. Contact the highest-scoring 20 first.

## 3. Reach out every day

Twenty personal messages a day beat 2,000 spammy ones. Use email, LinkedIn, Instagram DMs or WhatsApp (where appropriate), and walk into local businesses. The scripts are on the next pages.

## 4. Create content (optional, but it compounds)

Post what you build: a 30-second screen recording of the WhatsApp bot answering a voice note, a before/after of an invoice becoming a sheet row. **Workflow 07** turns one write-up into a week of posts. Content makes cold outreach warm, because people recognize you.

::: warning
**Stay legal and polite.** Follow the anti-spam and privacy rules where you and your prospects are (e.g. CAN-SPAM, GDPR/PECR, CASL). Send personal, relevant messages and always offer an easy way to say no. Never buy email lists. Never message people who opted out.
:::

## Follow-up (3–4 days later)

> Hi [First name], just bumping this in case it got buried. The demo trained on your site is still live: [Chat URL]. Ask it the question your customers ask most and see what it says. Happy to set it up properly if you like it.

## Walk-in / phone (local businesses)

> "Hi, I help [salons/clinics] in [area] answer WhatsApp messages automatically, even when you're busy with clients. Can I show you a 2-minute demo on my phone?"

## Referral ask (after a happy client)

> "I'm really glad the assistant is working well for you. Do you know one or two other [business owners] who are drowning in [messages/leads/invoices]? I'd be happy to give them a free demo."

# The discovery call ☎️ {#call}

Goal: understand the problem, put a number on it and agree on the next step. Talk 30% of the time and listen 70% of the time.

## Questions to ask

1. "Walk me through what happens when a new [enquiry/message/invoice] comes in today."
2. "How many do you get in a normal week? How quickly do you usually reply?"
3. "What happens to the ones you can't answer quickly?"
4. "Who does this today, and how many hours a week does it take?"
5. "What's a new customer worth to you on average?"
6. "If this was fully handled, what would you do with that time?"
7. "Have you tried anything before? What didn't work?"
8. "Who else is involved in a decision like this?"

## Put a number on it

> **Example (clinic):** 40 WhatsApp enquiries a week, 25% answered late. If even **2 extra patients a month** book because of faster replies, at ₹3,000 / $150 each, that's ₹6,000 / $300 a month. The care plan pays for itself, and the receptionist saves about 5 hours a week.

> **Example (accountant):** 300 invoices a month at 2 minutes each is **10 hours a month** of typing. At ₹500 / $25 per hour that's ₹5,000 / $250 a month, before counting fewer errors.

Close with: *"Based on what you told me, I'd suggest [package]. I can have it running within [timeframe]. Shall I send you a one-page proposal?"*

# Delivery checklist ✅ {#delivery}

## Before you start

- [ ] Signed proposal or agreement and the first payment received
- [ ] The client creates **their own** accounts and invites you: n8n, OpenAI (with credit), Google, Meta/WhatsApp
- [ ] You've collected the business info: services and prices, hours, FAQs, policies, booking link, tone of voice

## Build

- [ ] Import the workflow and connect the client's credentials (never yours)
- [ ] Fill in the Config node with their info and tone
- [ ] Test with **real examples**: their top 10 customer questions, edge cases, another language, an angry customer, a spam message
- [ ] Set up the **Error Alerts** bonus to email *you*
- [ ] Publish/activate it and test again with the production URL or number

## Handover

- [ ] Record a 5-minute screen video: what it does, where to see results, how to switch it off
- [ ] Share where everything lives (the n8n link, the sheets, which accounts are used)
- [ ] Agree on how the client tells you about changes (new prices, new services)
- [ ] Book a check-in for 7 days later

## Every month (care plan)

- [ ] Check executions and errors and fix anything that failed
- [ ] Review 10–20 real AI conversations and improve the prompts and business info
- [ ] Retrain the website chatbot if the site changed
- [ ] Send the monthly report (numbers + one improvement you made)

# Tools, setup & good practice 🧰 {#tools}

**One n8n per client, or one for everyone?** For most clients, use **their own n8n** (n8n Cloud or a small server) with **their own credentials**. Their data stays separate, they own it, and nothing breaks for others if one client leaves. For tiny clients, a shared instance you manage can work, but keep the credentials separate and be clear about it in your agreement.

**Keep your own templates.** Duplicate the workflows into a "master" folder and save your improvements there, such as better prompts for your niche and extra steps. Your library becomes more valuable with every client.

**Write things down.** For each client, keep a one-page sheet listing which workflows run, which accounts are connected, the Config values and the monthly fee.

## Contracts, data and AI ethics

- Use a simple **service agreement**: scope, price, payment terms, what's not included, the notice period for the care plan, and who owns the accounts and data (the client).
- If you handle personal data of EU/UK residents, a **data processing agreement** (DPA) may be required. Many countries have similar rules (for example, India's DPDP Act).
- Be transparent: tell end customers they're talking to an **AI assistant**, and always offer a way to reach a human (the WhatsApp bot does this with `human_handoff`).
- Follow the platform rules (Meta/WhatsApp Business policies, Google API terms) and **never promise results** you don't control, like "+50% sales".
